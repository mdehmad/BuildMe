        How BuildMe follows CDA bylaws

        BuildMe is a rule-based assistant. Every plan is built from an editable scheme: the rooms in each row, front to back, and how deep each row is. The same scheme always gives the same plan. Suggestions come from searching many schemes and keeping the best; after that, you're in control.

        Schedule A — bulk & height

        Rear open space follows current CDA practice: 5'-0" up to 5 marla, 7'-0" up to 1 kanal, 10'-0" for 1 kanal and above. All types: max 2 storeys and 30'-0" from the front road; parapet 3'-0". The whole structure, measured externally, counts as covered area.

        Other rules applied

        • Car porch (12.06): terraced single porch 180 ft² with at most 80 ft² in the setback; detached single 200 / 300 ft², double 300 / 400 ft². Min width 9'-0". Area within the limit is excluded from FAR. The porch roof is not a terrace.
        • Stair tower (Schedule B): 150 ft² (terraced ≤ 200 yd²), 200 ft² (201–625 yd²), 250 ft² (detached).
        • Servant room (cl. 10): required with a bath from 501 yd².
        • Minimum spaces (Annex 1): habitable rooms 100 ft² and 8'-6" wide; kitchen 45 ft² and 5'-0"; bath 24 ft² and 3'-0"; powder 15 ft².
        • Living units (cl. 16): one below 488 yd², two from 488 yd².

        Limits

        These are the CDA 1993 regulations as published on cda.gov.pk, plus current rear-setback practice. Later amendments and society bylaws (DHA, Bahria, B-17 and others) may differ: use "My society has different bylaws" on the review step. A licensed architect should prepare drawings for approval.

        Source: CDA Residential Sectors Zoning (Building Control) Regulation 1993 (PDF)
